RUNEHOLD

A duel of sigil cards. Four suits, one table, no mercy.

You and Maren the Tidebound each hold 30 Ward. Spend mana to play cards, guard yourself, heal, and chain suits for Resonance — reduce her Ward to zero before she reduces yours.

How to play

  • Your turn: mana refills and grows by 1 each turn (up to 10). Draw a card, then play as many as you can afford.
  • Attack (⚔) damages the rival's Ward. Guard (🛡) soaks damage until your next turn. Heal (✚) restores Ward.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same suit in a row and the second deals bonus damage.
  • Win by reducing Maren's Ward to 0. Lose if yours reaches 0.
  • The Long Night: from turn 12, both duellists lose Ward each turn (1, then 2, then 3…) — no duel stalls forever.
  • Mouse: click a card to select it, click again (or double-click) to play it. Keyboard: 1–7 select, Enter plays, E ends the turn.

An original game — its cards, suits, art and sounds were made for Runehold. Runs entirely offline, no network.
